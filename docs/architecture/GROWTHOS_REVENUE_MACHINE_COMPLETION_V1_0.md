# GrowthOS Revenue Machine Completion v1.0

Status: frozen for Sprint 063 Product Review

## Purpose

GrowthOS turns a reviewed prospect and immutable evidence into a human-controlled revenue
opportunity. The AI Growth Consultant is an advisory workflow, not a sales actor:

Prospect → Evidence → Growth Diagnosis → Opportunity → Growth Gift → Offer Recommendation →
Outreach Draft → Reply Analysis → Sales Strategy → Learning.

## Ownership and authority

- GrowthOS owns prospect research, diagnoses, gifts, outreach drafts, and conversation advice.
- Industry Intelligence owns reusable, evidence-backed vertical knowledge.
- Governance owns approval and external communication authority.
- AI Runtime supplies governed analysis and draft provenance only.
- Learning Loop consumes accepted observations without modifying prospect or evidence truth.

No diagnosis, recommendation, draft, or reply analysis may send a message, approve an action,
promise an outcome, move money, or mutate external systems.

## Growth diagnosis

A diagnosis records the observed business situation, specific growth problems, cited prospect
evidence, customer impact, improvements, confidence, risks, and optional governed AI request.
Every evidence reference must belong to the same organization and prospect. Unsupported revenue,
ROI, or outcome guarantees are rejected.

## Growth Gift and offer recommendation

Growth Gifts retain the existing human approval lifecycle and add a diagnosis link, personalized
diagnosis, before/after concept, recommended improvement, implementation scope, and customer value
explanation. They remain specific to one prospect.

Offer recommendations use deterministic, versioned rules over explicit inputs. Multi-location,
high-review/weak-visibility, new-business, and existing-business inputs map to Expansion,
Visibility, Launch Growth, and Growth Optimization packages respectively. Missing facts are not
invented, and the output is advisory.

## Outreach and conversation intelligence

One reviewed outreach record retains founder-friendly, consultant, and gift-first versions.
Existing evidence, Gift approval, RBAC, audit, and human-send boundaries remain mandatory.

Sales Copilot classifies replies as interested, question, price objection, not now, or referral.
It stores a suggested response, next action, and response risk as a draft. A human decides whether
and how to reply.

## Industry delivery knowledge

Reusable Beauty knowledge supports website optimization, GEO, social content, review improvement,
and common objections. Each record cites immutable evidence belonging to its industry profile and
is tenant scoped and version labelled. The same contract supports future industries without a new
knowledge engine.

## Security and non-goals

All APIs use the existing authentication, organization scope, RBAC, and audit boundaries. This
foundation adds no autonomous sales agent, automatic sending, negotiation, payment execution,
customer contact, or SaaS packaging.
